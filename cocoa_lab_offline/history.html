可可樹的完美氣候條件

The Perfect Climate for Cocoa Trees

可可豆（Cocoa Beans）非常嬌貴，只能生長在極度特定的氣候帶。請在右側調整模擬器，選出合適的環境參數，成功培育你的可可樹！
Cocoa beans are highly sensitive. Please adjust the simulator on the right to match the exact environmental requirements and grow your cocoa tree!

💡 學習提示 (Learning Hints)

  • 可可樹生長在溫暖且濕潤的熱帶雨林。
    Grows in warm, humid tropical rainforests.
  • 它對低溫極度敏感，冷風會使其落葉。
    Extremely sensitive to low temperatures.
  • 水分是關鍵，必須全年雨量充足且分布均勻。
    Requires abundant and well-distributed rainfall.
  • 地理位置局限在赤道附近。
    Restricted to areas near the Equator.
