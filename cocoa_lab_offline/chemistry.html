The structure and properties of chocolate 巧克力的結構與特性

Investigate how melting chocolate changes its structure and affects properties like taste, texture and melting point.
探討融化巧克力如何改變其結構，並影響口感、質地與熔點等特性。
The structure of a substance affects its properties, and this is also true for chocolate. In this experiment, students explore what happens to chocolate when it is melted and allowed to reharden, testing the taste, texture and melting point of the chocolate to determine how these properties have changed as a result of the change in structure.
物質的結構會影響其特性，這點在巧克力上也不例外。在這個實驗中，學生將探索巧克力融化並重新固化後會發生什麼事，透過測試巧克力的口感、質地與熔點，來判斷這些特性如何因結構改變而產生變化。

Equipment 實驗器材

Apparatus 儀器

  • Boiling tubes, x2
    試管 2 支
  • Beaker, 250 cm³ (or a similar-sized container)
    燒杯 250 cm³（或類似大小的容器）
  • Thermometer, 0-100°C
    溫度計 0-100°C
  • Timer
    計時器
  • Access to a kettle (for boiling water)
    熱水壺（用於準備沸水）

Chemicals 化學材料

  • Milk chocolate
    牛奶巧克力
  • Milk chocolate that has been melted and rehardened
    融化後重新固化的牛奶巧克力

Procedure 實驗步驟

Taste tests 試吃測試

Take two pieces of chocolate, one that has been melted and rehardened and one that has not. Note the differences between the two. Try snapping the pieces and see what happens. Then eat the two pieces separately and note any differences in taste and texture.
取兩塊巧克力，一塊是融化後重新固化的，另一塊則是未處理過的。請注意兩者之間的差異。試著將它們折斷，看看會發生什麼事。接著分別品嚐這兩塊巧克力，並記錄在味道與質地上的任何不同之處。

Melting point tests 熔點測試

  1. Put some hot water (at no more than 50°C) into the beaker.
    將一些熱水（溫度不超過 50°C）倒入燒杯中。
  2. Place a few small pieces of chocolate (enough to cover the bulb of a thermometer when melted) into a boiling tube and put in a thermometer.
    將幾小塊巧克力（融化後的量需足以覆蓋溫度計底部）放入試管中，並插入溫度計。
  3. Take the temperature of the chocolate, then put the boiling tube into the hot water and start the timer.
    測量巧克的初始溫度，然後將試管放入熱水中並開始計時。
  4. Stir continuously with the thermometer and record the temperature of the chocolate every 15-30 seconds for about five minutes. Note any other changes.
    用溫度計持續攪拌，在約五分鐘的時間內，每 15 到 30 秒記錄一次巧克力的溫度。注意觀察是否有其他變化發生。
  5. Repeat steps 1-4 with chocolate that has been melted and rehardened.
    使用融化後重新固化的巧克力，重複上述步驟 1 到 4。
  6. Draw a graph of each set of results and use them to decide the melting point of the samples and if the samples have the same structure.
    為每一組結果繪製圖表，並利用這些圖表來決定樣品的熔點，同時判斷這些樣品是否具有相同的結構。

Questions 課後問題

  1. What is the melting point of milk chocolate? Which polymorph is present in the sample?
    牛奶巧克力的熔點是多少？樣品中存在哪一種同質易形體？
  2. What is the melting point of the rehardened milk chocolate? Which polymorph is present in the sample?
    重新固化後的牛奶巧克力熔點是多少？樣品中存在哪一種同質易形體？
  3. Are your results similar as your prediction? Comment on your results.
    你的實驗結果與預測相似嗎？請對你的結果提出評論。

Chocolate Fondue Fun 巧克力鍋樂趣

Chocolate fondue is a fun and delicious dessert that is perfect for sharing with family and friends. In this procedure, you will learn how to make simple chocolate fondue using melted chocolate and a few easy ingredients. Then, you can dip fruits, marshmallows, and other snacks into the warm chocolate and enjoy!
巧克力鍋是一道有趣又美味的甜點，非常適合與家人和朋友分享。在這個製作過程中，你將學會如何使用融化的巧克力與幾樣簡單的食材來製作簡易巧克力鍋。接著，你可以將水果、棉花糖和其他零食沾入溫熱的巧克力中好好享用！

Ingredients 準備食材

For the dipping fondue chocolate: 巧克力鍋底：

  • 1 cup (8 ounces) heavy cream, Pinch salt
    1 杯（8 盎司）鮮奶油，少許鹽
  • 12 ounces milk or dark chocolate (chips or roughly chopped bar)
    12 盎司牛奶或黑巧克力（巧克力豆或切塊的巧克力磚）

For dipping: 沾醬配料：

  • Strawberries
    草莓
  • Banana pieces cut into 1-inch chunks
    切成 1 英寸塊狀的香蕉
  • Marshmallows
    棉花糖

Method 製作方法

  1. Heat the cream: Heat the cream with a pinch of salt over medium heat in a small saucepan until tiny bubbles show and begins to lightly and slowly simmer.
    加熱鮮奶油：在小湯鍋中以中火加熱鮮奶油與少許鹽，直到邊緣出現微小氣泡並開始緩慢微滾。
  2. Remove from heat and add the chocolate: Remove from heat, add the chocolate, and whisk until smooth and fully incorporated.
    離火並加入巧克力：將湯鍋移開火源，加入巧克力，並攪拌至滑順且完全融合。
  3. Serve immediately: Transfer the chocolate mix to a fondue pot heated at low or with a low flame, or serve straight from the pot. Arrange the dip-ables on a platter or plates around the chocolate pot. Use a bamboo skewer to dip the fruit pieces and other dip-ables into the hot melted chocolate mixture. Eat immediately.
    立即享用：將巧克力混合物倒入以小火或微火加熱的巧克力鍋中，或是直接從湯鍋中享用。將準備好的配料擺放在盤子上並環繞巧克力鍋。用竹籤串起水果塊與其他配料，沾取熱騰騰的融化巧克力糊。馬上吃一口吧！
Source: Simply Recipes, simplyrecipes.com
