Paragraph 1 夏末的寧靜與新學年的挑戰（A Fresh Start & Challenges）
課本 p. 4 · 破題段
For two months every summer, many high school buildings around the world are quiet. You can walk down an empty hallway, and the only sound that you hear is your own footsteps. However, this changes when a new school year begins, and these buildings are filled with activity once again. At this time, students return to school, meet their teachers, talk with their friends, and get ready to learn again. Indeed, the first day of high school is a fresh start for everyone. For most students, it's exciting. For first-year students, though, the first day of high school can be a little scary. This could be due to the challenge of having to learn a new class schedule in an unfamiliar building. After all, not everyone can easily fit in at a new school. Because of these concerns, teachers around the world try to make their new students feel welcome on the first day of high school.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
每年夏天有整整兩個月的時間，世界各地的許多高中校舍都很安靜。走在空蕩的走廊上，你聽見唯一的聲音就是自己的腳步聲。然而，當新學年開始，這種情況就改變了，這些建築物再次充滿了活力。此時，學生們返回學校，見見老師，和朋友聊天，並準備好重新學習。確實，高中的第一天對每個人來說都是個嶄新的開始。對大多數學生而言，這是令人興奮的。然而，對高一新生來說，高中第一天可能有點可怕。這可能是因為必須在陌生的大樓裡摸索新課表的挑戰。畢竟，不是每個人都能輕易融入新學校。正因如此，世界各地的老師們都在開學第一天盡力讓新生感受到熱烈的歡迎。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 對比鋪陳 (Contrast)：以暑假的安靜空寂對比開學後的熱鬧喧囂，營造強烈的開學儀式感。
  • 焦點轉移 (Shift of Focus)：由一般學生的興奮期待，轉入高一新生（first-year students）內心的焦慮與恐懼。
  • 主旨呼應 (Central Theme)：點出全球教師為了緩解新生的不安，紛紛創造多元溫馨的迎新傳統。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)
  • be filled with / be full of 充滿了……
  • a fresh start 嶄新的開始
  • due to / because of / owing to 由於；因為（接名詞/V-ing）
  • fit in (at/with) 融入環境；合得來
  • make sb. feel welcome 讓某人感到賓至如歸
🎯 克漏字語法焦點 (Cloze & Grammar)
  • the only + N + that...：受 the only 修飾時，先行詞關係代名詞習慣用 that（不用 which）。
  • 副詞 though 放在句中插入語位置，表「然而、不過」（等同 however）。
  • 部分否定：not everyone can easily fit in（並非每個人都能輕易融入，非全部否定）。
  • 使役動詞句型：make + O + 原形動詞 (feel) + adj. (welcome)。

Paragraph 2 美國高中的創意迎新（Creative Traditions in the United States）
課本 p. 4 · 舉例說明段
The following are some examples of this from the United States. At a high school in Arizona, for example, students get to walk down a red carpet as they enter the school on the first day, just like celebrities at an awards ceremony. In 2016, a math teacher in Michigan sang pop songs to her students but replaced the old lyrics with her own about classroom rules. This method definitely caught her students' attention and helped them learn the rules. In California, a teacher asked his students a simple question about their futures: “What is your hope?” Then, he had the students write down their answers and talk about them in groups. All of these experiences above can help to bring the new students together and raise everyone's spirits.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
下列是一些來自美國的例子。舉例來說，在亞利桑那州的一所高中，學生在開學第一天踏進校園時可以走紅地毯，就像在頒獎典禮上的明星一樣。在 2016 年，密西根州一位數學老師為學生演唱流行歌曲，但把原本的歌詞換成她自己寫的班級常規。這種方式毫無疑問吸引了學生的注意，並幫助他們記住規則。在加州，一位老師問了他的學生一個關於未來的簡單問題：「你的期望是什麼？」接著，他讓學生寫下答案並分組討論。以上這些經驗都能幫助凝聚新生，並提振每個人的精神。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 並列實例論證 (Parallel Examples)：連續列舉亞利桑那州（紅地毯）、密西根州（改編班規流行歌）、加州（分享未來希望）三大具體生動案例。
  • 層次推進 (Progression)：從外在視覺儀式（紅地毯）➔ 課堂互動趣味（流行歌）➔ 內在心理共鳴（夢想交流）。
  • 段落小結 (Sub-conclusion)：歸納三大活動的核心效益——凝聚新生（bring together）與鼓舞精神（raise spirits）。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)
  • get to + V 得以……；有機會做……
  • walk down a red carpet 走過紅地毯
  • replace A with B 用 B 取代 A
  • catch sb.'s attention 吸引某人的注意力
  • raise sb.'s spirits 提振某人的精神
🎯 克漏字語法焦點 (Cloze & Grammar)
  • 倒裝句型：The following are + 複數名詞 (some examples)（動詞與後面主詞一致）。
  • 使役動詞：have + O + 原形動詞 (write down) and (talk about)。
  • 助動詞用法：help + (to) + 原形動詞，可省略 to。

Paragraph 3 全球多元文化習俗（First-Day Traditions Across the Globe）
課本 p. 5 · 國際文化拓展段
The United States is not the only country with such interesting traditions. In India, for instance, the beginning of the school year often falls during the monsoon, so students exchange umbrellas to make new friends. In Russia and some countries in eastern Europe, the first day of school is called “Knowledge Day,” and seniors often perform songs to encourage the new students. Likewise, in New Zealand, senior students perform a war dance that is called “haka” on the first day of school. This gets new students excited to learn.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
美國並非唯一擁有如此有趣傳統的國家。舉例來說，在印度，新學年的開始正好碰上季風雨季，因此學生們會交換雨傘來結交新朋友。在俄羅斯和一些東歐國家，開學第一天被稱為「知識日」，高年級學長姐經常表演歌曲來鼓勵新生。同樣地，在紐西蘭，高年級學生會在開學第一天表演被稱為「哈卡（haka）」的毛利戰舞。這讓新生對即將開始的學習感到無比興奮。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 轉折過渡 (Transition)：從美國視角延伸至全球不同地理與文化脈絡。
  • 跨文化案例 (Cross-Cultural Variety)：印度（氣候適應：換傘交友）、俄羅斯/東歐（知識節日：歌唱祝福）、紐西蘭（原住民文化：哈卡戰舞振奮士氣）。
  • 同中求異 (Universal Warmth)：形式雖截然不同，但促進連結、消弭恐懼的核心價值全球共通。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)
  • fall during / fall on 正好落在（特定時期/日期）
  • exchange A for B / exchange A 交換 A
  • make new friends 結交新朋友
  • perform a dance/song 表演舞蹈／歌曲
  • get sb. excited / get sb. to V 使某人感到興奮／促使某人做……
🎯 克漏字語法焦點 (Cloze & Grammar)
  • 轉折副詞：Likewise / Similarly 表「同樣地；正如上述」。
  • 關係代名詞簡化：a war dance that is called “haka” ➔ 可簡化為 a war dance called “haka”。
  • 使役動詞用法：get + O + adj. (excited) 或 get + O + to V（注意 get 接主動動詞要加 to，接受詞被動感受用 p.p.）。

Paragraph 1 無聊午後的丟石遊戲（A Careless Game of Stones）
課本 p. 24 · 背景引言段
When I was a kid, I delivered newspapers in my community every day. One Saturday afternoon, my friend and I were bored. As a result, we randomly picked an elderly lady's house to be part of our game of throwing stones. To us, the stones turned into missiles as they rolled to the roof's edge and shot out into the yard. They also looked like meteorites that fell from the sky.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
當我還是個孩子時，我每天都在我的社區送報紙。某個週六下午，我和朋友感到很無聊。因此，我們隨機選了一位老婦人的房子，當作我們丟石頭遊戲的一部分。對我們來說，當石頭滾到屋頂邊緣並飛射進院子時，彷彿變成了飛彈。它們看起來也像是從天而降的隕石。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 背景設定 (Setting the Scene)：交代主角兒時送報的日常，以及無聊週末孩子們輕率無知的玩心。
  • 生動譬喻 (Figurative Imagery)：將屋頂滾落的碎石比喻為飛彈（missiles）與天降隕石（meteorites），凸顯孩童脫離現實的遊戲心態。
  • 伏筆埋設 (Foreshadowing)：以「隨機挑選老婦人的房子」為後續意外打破玻璃的衝突埋下種子。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)
  • deliver newspapers 送報紙
  • as a result / consequently 結果；因此
  • randomly pick / at random 隨機挑選
  • turn into / transform into 轉變成……
  • shoot out into 疾射衝進……
🎯 克漏字語法焦點 (Cloze & Grammar)
  • 時間副詞片語：One Saturday afternoon（特定週六下午不加 on）。
  • 動詞片語：turn into 表性質或外觀轉變。
  • 感官動詞/連綴動詞：look like + N (meteorites)。

Paragraph 2 意外打破玻璃與驚惶逃跑（The Broken Window & Escape）
課本 p. 24 · 衝突觸發段
I found a stone and sent it for a ride to the lady's roof. However, the stone was too smooth for me to grasp firmly, so it slipped out of my hand when I threw it. To my shock, it hit a small window in the lady's house. On hearing the crash, we decided to escape immediately.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
我找了一顆石頭，將它拋向老婦人的屋頂。然而，那顆石頭太過光滑以至於我無法牢牢抓緊，所以當我投擲時它從我手中滑脫了。令我震驚的是，它擊中了老婦人家的一扇小窗戶。一聽到破碎的巨響，我們便立刻決定逃之夭夭。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 意外發生 (The Accident)：石頭太滑（too smooth to grasp firmly）導致出手失控，釀成大禍。
  • 瞬間恐懼 (Immediate Reaction)：巨響傳來，孩子本能的第一反應是逃避責任、迅速逃跑。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)
  • send sth. for a ride 讓某物飛馳而出
  • grasp firmly / hold tight 抓緊
  • slip out of sb.'s hand 從某人手中滑落
  • to sb.'s shock / amazement 令某人震驚的是
  • on hearing / upon hearing 一聽到……就……

Paragraph 3 微塑膠與濃湯毒素（Microplastics & Toxic Soup）
課本 p. 115 · 科學機制段
Plastic is durable, which means it does not biodegrade easily. Instead, over hundreds of years, ultraviolet sunlight and pounding waves break it down into tiny fragments known as microplastics. These particles turn clear waters into what environmentalists call “plastic soup.” Even worse, microplastics act like chemical sponges, absorbing poisonous industrial pollutants in the water and becoming toxic ticking time bombs.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
塑膠非常耐用，這意味著它不易生物分解。相反地，歷經數百年，紫外線陽光和海浪的拍打將其分解成被稱為「微塑膠」的微小碎片。這些顆粒將原本清澈的海水變成了環保人士所稱的「塑膠濃湯」。更糟糕的是，微塑膠就像化學海綿一樣，吸附水中具有毒性的工業污染物，變成了帶毒的定時炸彈。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 材質矛盾 (Material Paradox)：耐用性（durable）原本是塑膠優點，在自然界卻成了萬年不化的惡夢。
  • 降解迷思釐清 (Degradation vs. Biodegradation)：陽光與海浪只是將大塑膠打碎成微塑膠（microplastics），並未真正消失。
  • 形象譬喻 (Vivid Metaphors)：運用「塑膠濃湯（plastic soup）」與「化學海綿（chemical sponges）」，極致呈現看不見的毒害。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)
  • break down into 分解成……
  • microplastics 微塑膠
  • plastic soup 塑膠濃湯（漂浮大量微塑膠的海域）
  • act like a chemical sponge 如同化學海綿般吸收毒素
  • even worse / what is worse 更糟糕的是
🎯 克漏字語法焦點 (Cloze & Grammar)
  • 非限定關係子句：..., which means it does not biodegrade easily（which 代替前述整件事）。
  • 複合關係代名詞：turn clear waters into what environmentalists call “plastic soup”（what = the thing which）。
  • 分詞片語後位修飾：tiny fragments (known as microplastics)。

Paragraph 4 海洋生物的慘烈悲歌（Marine Victims & Starvation）

Both the ocean and marine life are now paying a terrible price. Sea turtles mistake floating plastic bags for jellyfish, their favorite meal. 🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：

🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 生動悲劇細節 (Tragic Wildlife Details)：海龜誤食塑膠袋、海鳥餵食幼鳥瓶蓋，具體悲情擊中人心。

課本 p. 163 · 數字禁忌段
Nowhere is superstition more glaring than in our irrational reactions to numbers. In Western societies, the number thirteen is intensely dreaded—a phobia known as triskaidekaphobia. Many high-rise hotels and airplanes skip the 13th floor or 13th row altogether, fearing that bad luck will strike. Conversely, in Chinese culture, the number four is strictly shunned because its pronunciation sounds identical to the word for “death.” Hospitals rarely label a fourth floor, and apartment prices on the 4th floor are noticeably discounted. Meanwhile, eight is revered as lucky because it rhymes with “wealth and prosperity.”
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：
沒有什麼地方比我們對數字的非理性反應更能明顯體現迷信了。在西方社會，數字 13 令人格外恐懼——這種恐懼症被稱為「十三恐懼症」。許多高樓飯店和飛機直接跳過 13 樓或第 13 排座位，害怕厄運降臨。相反地，在華人文化中，數字 4 則遭到嚴格避諱，因為它的發音與「死」字完全相同。醫院極少標示 4 樓，而 4 樓的公寓房價也往往有明顯折扣。與此同時，8 則被尊崇為吉祥數字，因為它與「發財、富貴」押韻。
🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 否定倒裝句強調 (Negative Inversion for Emphasis)：Nowhere is superstition more glaring than... 開篇氣勢磅礡。
  • 東西方對照 (East-West Contrast)：西方恐懼 13（跳過 13 樓）vs. 東方避諱 4（諧音忌死）與推崇 8（諧音發財）。
  • 現實商業影響 (Real-World Economic Impact)：迷信直接影響建築樓層標示、飛機排位與房屋買賣價格。
🔗 核心必考搭配詞 (Collocations)

  • triskaidekaphobia 十三恐懼症
  • skip the 13th floor 跳過第 13 樓
  • shun the number four 避諱數字四

Paragraph 5 碎鏡與灑鹽的破煞解方（Broken Mirrors & Spilled Salt）

🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)

Paragraph 5 絕望復仇與致命宿命（From Sorrow to Vengeance）
課本 p. 214 · 悲劇激化段
Repeated cruelty turns innocence into venom. Realizing that humanity will never accept him, the heartbroken creature declares everlasting war against all mankind, and particularly against his negligent maker. He tracks down Frankenstein and makes a solemn plea: create an equally deformed female companion so that he will not have to suffer eternal solitude. Victor initially agrees, but then tears the second creation to shreds, terrified of breeding a race of monsters. Maddened with grief, the creature vows: “I shall be with you on your wedding night.” True to his curse, he murders Victor's bride Elizabeth, sealing their mutual doom.

Paragraph 6 北極冰原的終局追獵（The Arctic Chase & Final Mourning）

Paragraph 7 誰才是真正的怪物？（Who Is the Real Monster?）
課本 p. 215 · 終極省思段
Shelley's masterpiece leaves readers with an unforgettable ethical dilemma: Who is the true monster in this story? Is it the grotesque creature who murdered out of unbearable loneliness and rejection, or is it Victor Frankenstein, whose reckless ambition and unforgivable lack of compassion unleashed catastrophe? In our modern era, as scientists create artificial intelligence, gene editing, and humanoid robotics, the warning of Frankenstein glows brighter than ever: knowledge without empathy is lethal, and creation without responsibility is a curse.
🇹🇼 中文流暢對照翻譯：

🏗️ 篇章與段落結構 (Discourse Structure)
  • 永恆提問 (The Ultimate Dilemma)：拋出核心詰問——誰才是真正的怪物？引導讀者進行高階思辨。
  • 對稱論辯 (Symmetrical Contrast)：被遺棄者的絕望反撲 vs. 傲慢造物者的冷血失職。
  • 世紀警鐘 (Prophetic Warning)：知識若無同理心便具毀滅性，創造若無責任心則淪為浩劫詛咒。
