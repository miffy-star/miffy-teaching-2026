濃度與利潤期望值

Concentration & Expected Profit

1. 濃度解密

Decoding Concentration

市面上標示 70% 的黑巧克力，代表剩下的 30% 是什麼？

For a dark chocolate labeled 70%, what does the remaining 30% consist of?

解答 Answer：
70% 包含「可可膏 (Cocoa Mass)」與「可可脂 (Cocoa Butter)」。剩下的 30% 通常是糖 (Sugar)、奶粉 (Milk Powder) 或香草 (Vanilla)。

練習題 Practice：

(答 Ans：65g)

2. 固定與變動成本

Fixed & Variable Costs

As a CEO, you must understand your cost structure before pricing:

🧮 期望值公式與定價情境

Expected Value Formula & Pricing Scenario

E(X) = (預期獲利 Profit × 成功機率 P_Success) + (預期虧損 Loss × 失敗機率 P_Fail)

假設每片巧克力成本為 30 元。試著比較以下兩種策略：

Assume the cost per chocolate bar is $30 NTD. Compare the following two strategies:

策略 A (精品高價：賣 100 元)
Strategy A (Premium High Price: Sell for $100 NTD)

單件獲利 Profit per unit：70 元 (NTD)
預估賣出機率 Est. Success Rate：30%
滯銷虧本機率 Est. Fail Rate (Loss -30)：70%
計算 Calc：(70 × 0.3) + (-30 × 0.7) = 0 元 (NTD)
策略 B (親民低價：賣 50 元)
Strategy B (Affordable Low Price: Sell for $50 NTD)

單件獲利 Profit per unit：20 元 (NTD)
預估賣出機率 Est. Success Rate：90%
滯銷虧本機率 Est. Fail Rate (Loss -30)：10%
計算 Calc：(20 × 0.9) + (-30 × 0.1) = 15 元 (NTD)

神聖的祭品與貨幣 (Xocolatl)

Sacred Offerings & Currency

Theobroma cacao (Theo=神 God，broma=食物 Food)。原住民將可可豆磨碎，加入「水、玉米粉、辣椒」，打出豐富的泡沫，用於宗教祭祀與戰士出征前飲用。

Indigenous people ground cocoa beans and mixed them with water, cornmeal, and chili to create a frothy drink for religious rituals and warriors.

當時，可可豆就是金錢，100 顆可可豆甚至可以買一隻火雞！

At the time, cocoa beans were money. 100 beans could even buy a turkey!

權力、糖與奴隸貿易

Power, Sugar & Slave Trade

西班牙征服者將可可帶回歐洲。歐洲人不喜歡苦辣味，於是加入了「糖 (Sugar)」與「肉桂」。喝熱巧克力成為皇室奢華與財富的象徵。

Spanish conquistadors brought cocoa to Europe. Disliking the bitter/spicy taste, Europeans added sugar and cinnamon. Drinking hot chocolate became a symbol of royal luxury and wealth.

但這份甜蜜背後，卻促成了悲慘的非洲奴隸貿易與中南美洲種植園經濟。

However, behind this sweetness lay the tragic African slave trade and the plantation economy in Central and South America.

供應鏈與公平貿易

Supply Chain & Fairtrade

100 元巧克力：殘酷利潤分配

$100 Bar: The Cruel Profit Distribution

可可帶 (The Cocoa Belt)

可可樹極度嬌貴，只能生長在赤道南北緯 20 度以內，需要高溫多雨的氣候。西非（如象牙海岸、迦納）包辦全球 60% 的可可產量。 然而，這些農民處於供應鏈的最底層。

Cocoa trees are extremely delicate and only grow within 20 degrees north and south of the equator, requiring a hot and rainy climate. West Africa (e.g., Ivory Coast, Ghana) accounts for 60% of global cocoa production. However, these farmers are at the very bottom of the supply chain.

🤝 公平貿易倡議

Fairtrade Initiative

為了解決剝削問題，公平貿易認證立下了嚴格的標準：

To combat exploitation, Fairtrade certification established strict standards:

  • 保證收購價 Guaranteed Minimum Price：
    保護農民免受國際期貨價格崩盤影響。
    Protects farmers from international market price crashes.
  • 禁止童工 No Child Labor：
    終結數百萬兒童在可可園砍伐果莢的悲劇。
    Ends the tragedy of millions of children working in cocoa farms.
  • 社區發展基金 Community Premium：
    提撥部分利潤回饋當地社區，建立學校與水井。
    Allocates extra funds back to communities to build schools and wells.

發酵魔法與快樂大腦

Fermentation Magic & The Happy Brain

🦠

發酵：美味的起點

Fermentation: The Origin of Flavor

可可豆剛採下時被白色果肉包覆，毫無巧克力味。真正的魔法發生在發酵箱裡：

Freshly harvested cocoa beans are covered in white pulp with zero chocolate flavor. The real magic happens in the fermentation box:

  • Day 1-2:
    酵母菌 (Yeast) 啟動，將糖分轉化為酒精。
    Yeast converts sugars into alcohol.
  • Day 3-4:
    乳酸與醋酸菌 (Bacteria) 產生酸與高溫（達50度），殺死種子。
    Bacteria produce acid and high heat (up to 50°C), killing the seed.
  • Day 5-7:
    蛋白質分解，產生巧克力香氣的前驅物質。
    Proteins break down, creating flavor precursors.

Chocolate is rich in Tryptophan. It's an essential amino acid our bodies cannot produce, acting as the raw material for the brain's "happiness chemical".

Chef's Kitchen: Emulsification & Texture

⚠️ 隔水加熱法

Double Boiler

直火加熱會燒焦。必須將巧克力放在鋼盆中，置於微滾熱水鍋上，利用蒸氣溫和融化。切忌滴入純水，否則會瞬間結塊 (Seize)。

Direct heat burns it. Melt chocolate gently in a bowl over simmering water using steam heat. Never let a drop of water in, or it will seize instantly.

🥣 乳化作用

Emulsification

油（可可脂）與水（鮮奶油）本不相容。利用加熱攪拌與鮮奶油中的蛋白質作為介面活性劑，形成滑順的甘納許 (Ganache)。

Oil (cocoa butter) and water (heavy cream) don't mix naturally. By heating, stirring, and using cream proteins as surfactants, we form a smooth Ganache.

🍪 創造層次

美味的關鍵在於口感對比。將 Smooth (滑順的融化外層) 與 Crunchy (敲碎的消化餅乾核) 完美結合。

The key to deliciousness is texture contrast. Perfectly combine the Smooth melted exterior with a Crunchy crushed cookie core.

結晶、生物鹼與美拉德反應

Crystallization, Alkaloids & Maillard Reaction

🌡️ 晶體型態

Crystal Forms

可可脂 (Cocoa Butter) 是一種多晶型物質，共有六種結晶型態 (Form I ~ VI)。

Cocoa Butter is a polymorphic substance with six distinct crystal forms (Form I ~ VI).

  • ❌ Form I ~ IV: 結構不穩定，熔點低，容易在室溫融化變軟。
    Unstable structure, low melting point, easily softens at room temperature.
  • ✅ Form V (Type 5): 完美型態！熔點約 34°C (低於體溫 37°C)。擁有漂亮光澤、清脆折斷聲，且「只融你口，不融你手」。
    Perfect form! Melts around 34°C (below body temp 37°C). Glossy finish, crisp snap, "melts in your mouth, not in your hand."

🔥 美拉德反應

Maillard Reaction

在烘焙 (Roasting) 時，食物中的 胺基酸 (Amino Acids) 與 還原糖 (Reducing Sugars) 在 140°C 以上受熱，會產生深褐色物質與複雜香氣。

During roasting, amino acids and reducing sugars react under heat (140°C+) to produce brown pigments and complex aromatic compounds.

☠️ 生物鹼

Alkaloids

巧克力含「可可鹼 (Theobromine)」。對人體能提神；但對貓狗是致命毒藥！因為牠們缺乏代謝酵素。

Chocolate contains Theobromine. It stimulates humans but is a deadly poison to cats and dogs due to their lack of metabolizing enzymes.
