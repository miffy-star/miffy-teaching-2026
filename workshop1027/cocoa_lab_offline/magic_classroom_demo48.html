STAGE III: READING PARAGRAPH 4

Henrietta died in 1951 from cervical cancer. Before she died, a surgeon took samples of her lump regardless of the absence of her consent. These samples were then put into a test tube. To the researchers' surprise, Henrietta's cells reproduced an entire generation every twenty-four hours, and they never halted. They became the first immortal human cells ever grown in a laboratory.

Critical Thinking: Is it OK for doctors to take cells without asking?

STAGE IV: INVERSION (倒裝句)

💡 規則：否定副詞移到句首，助動詞要搬到主詞前！

示範 1：Surgeons seldom noticed her consent.
→ Seldom did surgeons notice her consent.
示範 2：People never knew the story.
→ Never did people know the story.
示範 3：We little pay attention to the dark side.
→ Little do we pay attention to the dark side.
示範 4：Doctors rarely encountered such immortal cells.
→ Rarely did doctors encounter such immortal cells.
