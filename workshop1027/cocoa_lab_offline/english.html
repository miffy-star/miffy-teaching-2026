The Golden Formula 開場白黃金公式

1. HOOK
誘餌 (預告片)

Like a movie trailer! Grab their attention immediately. 就像「電影預告片」！一秒抓住聽眾眼球。

A. Question:
Have you ever wondered...?
(你曾想過嗎？)
B. Fun Fact:
Did you know that...?
(你知道嗎...？)
C. Imagine:
Imagine a world where...
(想像一個世界...)

2. BRIDGE
橋樑 (導航儀)

Like a GPS. Guide them from your hook to your main topic. 就像「導航儀」。把剛剛的誘餌，連接到主題。

A. Problem:
However, we are facing...
(然而我們面臨...)
B. History:
This started when...
(這源自於...)
C. Personal:
For me, chocolate is...
(對我來說...)

3. THESIS
主旨宣言 (大爆雷)

The Spoiler! Tell the audience exactly what you will prove today. 就像「直接爆雷」！清楚告訴大家你今天要證明什麼。

The Best Sentence:
"Today, I will [show/prove] you..."
(今天，我將向你們證明/展示...)
"Today, I will show you why dark chocolate makes you smarter."

Fix the Opening! 拯救破碎的開場白

Discuss: Which is Hook, Bridge, and Thesis? 小組討論：哪句是誘餌(H)、橋樑(B)、主旨(T)？
