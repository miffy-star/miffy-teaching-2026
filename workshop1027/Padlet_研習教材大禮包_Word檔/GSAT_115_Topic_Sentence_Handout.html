Sample Essay

115學測圖片作文示範

Pets are playing an increasingly important role in people's lives. As the pictures show, people today do much more than simply feed or keep their pets at home. They spend time with them, care for them, and often treat them as close companions. Some pet owners even celebrate special occasions with their animals or take them along when they go out. These behaviors suggest that the relationship between humans and pets has changed. For many people, a pet is no longer just an animal but a beloved member of the family.

There are several reasons for this growing attachment to pets. One important reason is that animals can provide companionship and emotional support. For example, after a stressful day at school or work, spending time with a dog or cat may help a person feel calmer and less lonely. This close relationship can have positive effects because caring for an animal may bring comfort, happiness, and even a stronger sense of responsibility. However, owning a pet also requires time, money, and long-term commitment. Therefore, while pets can greatly enrich our lives, people should make sure they are ready to care for them responsibly.

兩段各自的任務

P1：What do the pictures SHOW?
描述重點細節 → 統整現象 → topic sentence
P2：Why? So what?
原因 → 例子／觀察 → 影響 → 結論
Drill 1

SEE → MEAN：不要停在圖片細節

Step 1 — SEE
Write 2–3 details you see.
Step 2 — THINK
What do these details suggest?

Choose the best topic sentence.

Drill 2

DETAIL → TOPIC → MAIN IDEA

What do you SEE?
What is the TOPIC?
What does it MEAN?

Write ONE topic sentence.

Reducing our dependence on disposable products is an important step toward solving the problem of plastic pollution.
Drill 3

兩張圖：找共同的大概念

1. What is different?
2. What bigger idea connects BOTH pictures?
3. Write a P1 topic sentence.
4. Write a P2 topic sentence.

P1: Students can learn effectively in different ways, either independently or through cooperation with others.

P2: Personally, I prefer studying alone because a quiet environment allows me to concentrate and work at my own pace.

Exit Ticket

只交兩句，不寫整篇

請根據任一組圖片，完成：

P1 Topic Sentence — What do the pictures SHOW?
P2 Topic Sentence — What do YOU THINK / EXPERIENCE?
